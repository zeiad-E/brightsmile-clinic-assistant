Your system asks for reduced motion. The effects still run here so you can judge them; the real site would swap them for simple fades.

21 · WebGL shader · signature candidate

Depth-map 2.5D

The real Lando Norris technique: one flat picture plus a grey "depth map". Every pixel moves by its own depth, so the scene bends like 3D. Press Depth map to see the secret ingredient.

How it's built
A raw WebGL fragment shader reads the depth map (white = near, black = far) and shifts each pixel by mouse × depth. No library, about 30 lines of GPU code.
vs. effect 08
08 moves six separate layers. This moves every pixel on its own, which gives smooth, round depth. On the real site, the picture would be your clinic photo with an AI-made depth map.
